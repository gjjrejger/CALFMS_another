ABOUT US

A Full-Service Law Firm

Chweya and Associates Advocates is a full-service law firm established in 2009 by its founding member, Amos Janai Chweya. The firm comprises advocates, legal consultants, paralegals, and administrative staff, and currently operates from Bidhaa Bora Buildings, next to Tower Sacco along Lumumba Street, Nanyuki.

Through a network of associate lawyers, the firm is able to handle legal matters across the country under one professional umbrella. Chweya and Associates Advocates is committed to providing clients with professional, amicable, effective, solution-oriented, and timely legal services, while continuously striving to build a strong reputation as a trusted and universally recognized full-service law firm.

OUR VISION

Practical Solutions. Informed Decisions.

Our firm's vision is to provide legal services to all businesses, institutions or persons, offer practical solutions to our clients and be universally recognized as a full-service law firm.

OUR COMMITMENT

We undertake to embrace our clients' needs and address them soundly by offering turnaround time to enable them to make informed decisions.

AREAS OF EXPERTISE & PRACTICE

Our Practice Areas

Chweya and Associates Advocates offers diverse expertise across major areas of law in Kenya.

01

Corporate & Commercial Law

Comprehensive legal counsel for businesses, corporate transactions, compliance and commercial matters.

VIEW DETAILS →

We provide comprehensive legal counsel for all commercial aspects of your business, allowing you to focus on day-to-day operations while we handle the legal side. Our services span company incorporation, regulatory compliance, mergers and acquisitions, tax advisory, and contract drafting.

We advise on complex transactions including sales of shares, businesses, and assets, as well as joint ventures and takeovers. We conduct thorough due diligence investigations and compile reports to protect our corporate clients' interests. Beyond dispute resolution, we proactively identify and eliminate legal risks in your operations, helping your business grow with confidence.

02

Property & Conveyancing Law

Legal support throughout the property lifecycle, from acquisition and development to registration, transactions and disputes.

VIEW DETAILS →

As Kenya's construction sector grows, we bring deep expertise in property and conveyancing law to support large-scale projects. We handle the full property lifecycle — from acquisition, development, and due diligence to the preparation and registration of transfers, mortgages, leases, and tenancy agreements.

Our team also navigates the regulatory landscape, including land registration, stamp duty, change-of-use approvals, and real estate taxation. When disputes arise, we represent clients in court and through alternative dispute resolution, always weighing each case on its merits for the most realistic outcome.

03

General Litigation & ADR

Representation across a wide range of disputes, with a focus on effective resolution through litigation, negotiation, mediation and arbitration.

VIEW DETAILS →

Our attorneys provide specialized legal representation across all areas of litigation, combining deep courtroom experience with sharp investigative skills to locate evidence and build compelling cases in our clients' best interests.

We handle a wide range of disputes including torts, negligence and personal injury claims, professional liability, bankruptcy, contract disputes, insurance claims, debt recovery, defamation, succession and estate matters, employment disputes, consumer protection, environmental and regulatory issues, intellectual property infringement, and fraud.

We also provide urgent interlocutory relief, appellate proceedings, and arbitration services. Every case is evaluated on its merits, and we pursue the most effective path to resolution — whether through negotiation, mediation, or full court proceedings.

04

Constitutional & Administrative Law

Advice and representation on constitutional rights, administrative law and challenges to governmental action.

VIEW DETAILS →

Constitutional matters carry profound weight, as they shape the rights and freedoms of every citizen and their interpretations are final — alterable only through formal amendment.

Our firm represents clients in bringing constitutional claims, defending against constitutional challenges, advising on constitutional amendments, and challenging the validity of statutes on grounds of unconstitutionality.

We also provide expert guidance on the interpretation and application of both constitutional and administrative law, ensuring our clients are protected at every level of governance.

05

Labour & Employment Law

Employment and labour advice covering compliance, employment contracts, restructuring and labour disputes.

VIEW DETAILS →

The firm advises clients on all aspects of employment and labour law, from ensuring statutory compliance to representing them in labour court disputes.

Services include guidance on termination, retrenchment, and corporate restructurings; drafting employment contracts and union agreements; and advising on terms of service, voluntary retirement schemes, and employee stock options.

06

Insurance Law

Advice on insurance policies, claims, regulatory compliance, risk management and insurance disputes.

VIEW DETAILS →

The firm advises clients on all aspects of insurance law, from policy interpretation and claims disputes to regulatory compliance and contract drafting.

Services span representation in arbitration and court proceedings, structuring reinsurance agreements, and guiding corporate and individual clients on risk management, liability allocation, and coverage matters across motor, health, and general insurance.

07

Family Law

Sensitive and discreet representation in family, matrimonial, child-related and domestic matters.

VIEW DETAILS →

The firm represents clients in all family law matters, including divorce, judicial separation, child custody and support, adoption, and domestic violence.

Our advocates handle these emotionally complex cases with sensitivity and discretion, prioritizing the protection of clients' rights and their children's best interests.

08

Taxation & Audit Law

Advice and representation on Kenyan taxation, tax compliance, audits, disputes and proceedings.

VIEW DETAILS →

The firm advises on the full spectrum of Kenyan tax law, including transfer pricing, income tax, capital gains, customs, and excise matters.

We represent and defend taxpayers during audits, before the Tax Tribunal and High Court, and in criminal tax prosecutions, ensuring their rights are protected at every stage.

Entertainment Law
