# Chweya & Associates Law Firm Management System (CALFMS)

## Overview

**Chweya & Associates Law Firm Management System (CALFMS)** is a planned digital platform for Chweya & Associates Advocates.

The project is being developed in stages.

The first stage focuses on building a professional public-facing law firm website. The second stage will introduce the internal Law Firm Management System (LFMS), which will provide staff with tools for managing clients, matters, documents, tasks, billing, appointments, users and other firm operations.

The public website and the management system will eventually form one integrated platform.

# Project Objectives

The overall objectives of CALFMS are to:

- Provide an organized platform for managing law firm operations.
