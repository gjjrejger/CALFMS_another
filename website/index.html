ABOUT US

A Full-Service Law Firm

Chweya and Associates Advocates is a full-service law firm established in 2009 by its founding member, Amos Janai Chweya. The firm comprises advocates, legal consultants, paralegals, and administrative staff, and currently operates from Bidhaa Bora Buildings, next to Sidian Bank along Lumumba Street, Nanyuki.

Through a network of associate lawyers, the firm is able to handle legal matters across the country under one professional umbrella. Chweya and Associates Advocates is committed to providing clients with professional, amicable, effective, solution-oriented, and timely legal services, while continuously striving to build a strong reputation as a trusted and universally recognized full-service law firm.

AREAS OF EXPERTISE & PRACTICE

Our Practice Areas

Chweya and Associates Advocates offers diverse expertise across major areas of law in Kenya.

Corporate & Commercial Law

Company incorporation, legal compliance, mergers and acquisitions, taxation, contracts, partnerships, business law and commercial transactions.

Property & Conveyancing Law

Property acquisition and development, real estate transactions, transfers, leases, mortgages, charges, land matters and property-related disputes.

General Litigation & ADR

Legal representation and advice in litigation, including civil matters, negligence claims, professional liability, torts and alternative dispute resolution.

Constitutional & Administrative Law

Constitutional and administrative advice, representation and matters concerning constitutional rights and challenges.

Labour & Employment Law

Employment and labour advice, compliance, employment contracts, termination, retrenchment, industrial relations and labour disputes.

Insurance Law

Advice on insurance legislation, regulatory compliance, policy liability, repudiated claims and liability assessment.

Family Law

Legal assistance in divorce, judicial separation, child custody and support, adoption and other family matters.

Taxation & Audit Law

Advice on taxation matters including transfer pricing, income tax, tax procedures, customs and excise, capital gains and corporate tax.

Intellectual Property Law

Protection and registration of intellectual property including patents, trademarks and copyrights, together with related disputes and internet law matters.

Probate, Succession & Trusts

Advice and representation concerning wills, trusts, succession and probate administration, including preservation and distribution of estates.

Banking & Finance Law

Legal advice for financial institutions, borrowers and businesses involving lending, regulatory matters, financing transactions and banking documentation.

Competition Law

Legal services relating to competition matters and the regulatory environment affecting businesses.

Contracts Law

Drafting, review and advice concerning agreements and other contractual arrangements.

Notarial Practice

Notarial services and preparation and handling of relevant legal instruments.

Government & Private Institutional Legal Frameworks

Legal frameworks and advisory services for government and private institutions.

Administrative & Public Law

Legal advice and representation concerning administrative and public law matters.

Energy Law

Legal services relating to energy-sector matters and applicable legal frameworks.

Legal & Governance Audit

Legal research, legal audit and governance advisory services.

Legal Consultancy

Consultancy and professional advice on matters of law.

Entertainment Law

Legal services concerning entertainment and related intellectual property matters.
